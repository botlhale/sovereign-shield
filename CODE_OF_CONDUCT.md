# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

SovereignShield is stewarded by 13668754 Canada Inc. We are committed to a professional, inclusive, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, education, nationality, appearance, disability, race, religion, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, or sexual orientation.

We expect participation to reflect the care appropriate to security, statistical standards, public institutions, and sovereign data systems.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of constructive participation include:

- treating people and differing technical viewpoints with respect;
- giving specific, evidence-based, and actionable feedback;
- accepting responsibility, correcting mistakes, and learning from them;
- protecting confidential information and reporting security concerns privately;
- distinguishing reproducible facts from assumptions or promotional claims; and
- focusing on the health of the project and its community.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualized language or attention;
- trolling, insults, personal or political attacks, or sustained disruption;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- knowingly submitting secrets, personal data, confidential institutional data, or proprietary datasets;
- pressuring anyone to disclose employer, client, security, or procurement information; and
- conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, code review, project events, and public spaces where someone is representing SovereignShield or 13668754 Canada Inc. as its steward.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that do not align with this policy. Responses may include a private correction, warning, temporary restriction, or permanent ban, depending on context, severity, and pattern of behavior.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's **Security** tab by selecting **Report a vulnerability** and prefixing the report title with `Code of Conduct`. Do not include sensitive conduct reports in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed by the project steward, and the privacy and safety of the reporter will be respected.
